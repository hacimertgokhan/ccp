# Code of conduct

We want CCP to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background or experience.

**Expected:** be respectful, assume good faith, give and accept constructive feedback,
focus on what is best for the project and its users.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults or personal attacks, discrimination, publishing
others' private information, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

Maintainers may edit, reject or remove comments, commits and issues that violate this code,
and may remove contributors who repeatedly do. To report a problem, contact the maintainer
through the email on their GitHub profile; reports are handled in confidence.

This policy is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant.
